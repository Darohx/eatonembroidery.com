Competitive pricing.
Always quoted upfront.

Every order is different — garment choice, stitch count, number of positions and quantity all affect the final price. We always quote in full before you commit to anything.

Stitch count

Quantity

The more you order, the better the per-item price. Volume discounts apply automatically from 25 items.
